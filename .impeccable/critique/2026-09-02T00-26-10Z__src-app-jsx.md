## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: Authored for the product, not a reskinned template. Distinct copy voice, dual-division structure expressed structurally, consistent hard offset-shadow hover signature across every interactive surface. Slips toward generic in the testimonial carousel (placeholder content) and unclaimed social links (linkedin.com, x.com root domains).

Deterministic scan: detect.mjs ran clean against App.jsx and SubmitProperty.jsx (exit 0, zero findings).

Visual overlays: unavailable this session — no browser automation tool exposed. Both assessments ran as source review.

## Overall Impression

Visual system is disciplined and specific — the gap is trust, not craft. Two of three lead-capture surfaces (Contact, newsletter) are non-functional, and Contact actively tells the user it worked when it didn't.

## What's Working

1. Hard offset-shadow hover system, consistently implemented across buttons, division cards, service tiles, and steps.
2. Baldo Mindset feature block — real named partner, real stat, working outbound link; earns the founder/network positioning.
3. SubmitProperty's idle/sending/sent/error state machine with localized errors and client-side image downscaling.

## Priority Issues

[P0] Contact form fabricates a success state with no backend
Why it matters: Contact.submit() only does console.info(...) then shows "Received." Every lead is silently lost while the visitor is told a human will follow up.
Fix: Wire to a real endpoint mirroring /api/submit-property; only show success on a real 2xx response.
Suggested command: /impeccable harden

[P1] "List a Property" has no top-level discovery path
Why it matters: Nav entry removed, footer never had one. Only routes are Portfolio's empty-state/board CTAs, requiring a scroll past five sections. Property owners/sellers are a named primary persona with no direct path.
Fix: Restore a footer link to /list; consider a lighter nav affordance for sellers.
Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

[P1] Placeholder-as-label pattern in Contact loses recognition support
Why it matters: Contact uses placeholder+aria-label with no visible label, unlike SubmitProperty's correct label/htmlFor pairing. Value disappears once typing starts.
Fix: Add visible labels to Contact matching SubmitProperty's pattern.
Suggested command: /impeccable harden

[P2] Social links point to unclaimed root domains
Why it matters: LinkedIn/X links go to bare platform homepages with no handle — reads as unfinished template, undercuts trust earned elsewhere.
Fix: Link real accounts or remove icons until they exist.
Suggested command: /impeccable polish

[P2] Deal-type select (5 options) and Services grid (6 items) exceed the 4-choice scan comfort threshold
Why it matters: Both sit past single-glance chunking size; Services grid gives no sub-grouping cue despite "six lanes, one desk" being core positioning copy.
Fix: Visually group Services into two triads (talent vs. realty).
Suggested command: /impeccable layout
